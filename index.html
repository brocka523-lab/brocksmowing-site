What do your fall leaf cleanups include?

Complete leaf removal, flower beds cleaned out, and around bushes, trees and tight areas. We haul the leaves away, or dump them in the woods on your property if you'd rather. Cleanups start at $150.

Do you just pick up leaf piles?

Yes. If you've already raked them up yourself, we'll load your leaf piles and haul them off. Send us a text with a photo and we'll give you a price.

When should I sign up for snow plowing?

Before the first snow. Plow routes fill up quickly, so reserve your spot now through the booking form or by calling or texting 715-431-0923.

How do I get an exact price?

Fill out the booking form, or call or text us. Every yard is different, so we confirm your exact price before any work starts. Quotes are always free.

What areas do you serve?

Grantsburg, Siren, Webster and the surrounding Burnett County lake country. Not sure if you're in our area? Just ask.

Are you insured and licensed?

Yes. We're fully insured, and we hold a Wisconsin commercial pesticide applicator license for weed spraying and fertilizer.

About us

In it for the lawn haul

We're not a here-today, gone-tomorrow outfit. Brock's Mowing & Services started in 2019 with one mower and a simple idea: do every job like it's your own yard. Today we serve more than 130 customers every season across Grantsburg, Siren, Webster and the lake country in between.

Brock still runs the business himself and works right alongside the crew, so the person you talk to is the person who makes sure it gets done right. We're proud to give back too, supporting Grantsburg School District scholarships for more than four years.
